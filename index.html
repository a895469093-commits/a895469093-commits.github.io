Tiktok video

Automatic Video Uploader — a personal productivity tool that automatically uploads and publishes videos to the owner's own TikTok account through the official TikTok Content Posting API.

How it works

1. The owner adds a video file and caption to a queue.
2. The tool authenticates the owner's TikTok account with Login Kit.
3. The video is uploaded via the Content Posting API (video.upload).
4. The video appears in the owner's TikTok app as a draft, where the owner edits and posts it.
